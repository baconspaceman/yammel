# Code of Conduct

The rules for how people treat each other in the Archipelago YAML Editor project: its issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run in its name.

## Our approach

This project exists to make a hobby easier for people. We want everyone, whatever their experience with Archipelago, programming or English, to be able to ask a question, report a problem or offer a change and be treated decently.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and be patient with questions, including ones that have been asked before.
- Be specific and kind when you give feedback on someone's work. Say what you would change and why.
- Accept feedback on your own work without taking it as an attack.
- Keep to the topic of the project in issues and pull requests.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information, and remove private details from files you attach.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including insults, mockery, threats, stalking, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination or hateful language about a person's race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, appearance or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and pasting secrets or credentials.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as making the project unwelcoming or unsafe.

## Reporting a problem

If someone is behaving in a way that breaks these rules, or you feel unsafe, please tell the maintainer privately.

- Open a private security advisory on the repository (the **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**: `https://github.com/baconspaceman/archipelago-yaml-editor/security/advisories/new`). Say in the first line that it is a conduct report, not a vulnerability.
- Or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile, `https://github.com/baconspaceman`.

Please include what happened, where (a link if it is public), and who was involved. Reports are handled privately, and the person who reports will not be named to the person reported without their agreement.

## What happens next

The maintainer will read the report, may ask questions, and will decide what to do. Depending on what happened, that can be a private reminder, a request to edit or remove a comment, a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces, or no action when nothing broke these rules. The maintainer can edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules.

If the report is about the maintainer, say so in the report and, if you do not feel able to use the options above, you can report to GitHub through its own reporting tools.

## Scope

This applies to every project space and to public spaces when someone is representing the project. It applies to everyone who takes part, including the maintainer.

## Security problems are different

A vulnerability in the app is not a conduct matter. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for those.
